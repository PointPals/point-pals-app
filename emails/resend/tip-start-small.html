Three chores and one kind moment is plenty for week one.
        

Start small, {{{first_name}}}

The families who stick with a marble jar have one thing in common: they start with less than they think.

This week, try just three chores and one kind moment per child. Make bed, teeth, plates to the bench — and one thing worth noticing, like sharing or being brave.

Notice it straight away. A marble that drops the moment your child does something says “I saw that” far louder than a tally at bedtime.

When those feel easy, add one more. Habits grow from wins, not from long lists.
